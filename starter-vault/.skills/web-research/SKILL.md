# Web Research

1. Define the question, freshness requirement, useful source types, and stopping condition.
2. Prefer primary sources: official documentation, standards bodies, research papers, source repositories, and first-party announcements.
3. Use multiple independent sources when a claim is disputed or consequential. Record publication date and event date separately.
4. Capture direct URLs and concise evidence; do not paste entire copyrighted pages into the Vault.
5. Label single-source claims, inference, unresolved disagreement, and information that may expire.
6. Store durable source summaries under `20-资料/` or link them from the active project. Promote a conclusion into a standard or decision only through the appropriate workflow.
7. Never place login sessions, Cookies, Tokens, or account identifiers in research notes. Authorization to browse does not authorize posting, messaging, purchasing, or publishing.
