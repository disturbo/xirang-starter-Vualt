# Ingest Source

1. Record source name, location or URL, acquisition date, access limits, and completeness.
2. Preserve the raw source separately from the summary. Do not silently rewrite the original.
3. Extract facts, roles, processes, fields, states, decisions, interfaces, and open questions that matter to the current objective.
4. Keep a source pointer for consequential claims: page, sheet, heading, timestamp, screenshot number, or document block.
5. Mark OCR, visual inference, and unverified interpretation distinctly.
6. When sources conflict, route to `review-contradictions`; do not choose a winner without evidence or an authorized decision.
7. Store reusable raw material under `20-资料/` and link it from the relevant project. Never ingest passwords, Tokens, Cookies, private keys, recovery codes, or unrelated personal data.

The output is a traceable source record or summary, not an automatic PRD or final decision.
